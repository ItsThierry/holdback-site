# Fairhold

Fairhold checks a rate confirmation on your phone: the pay it finds, and whether the file shows signs of editing. It also
keeps your settlements, loads, costs and bills, and shows what is safe to spend. For owner-operators. No account, and your
files stay on your phone.

## Support

Questions, a problem with the app, or a rate con it read wrong: email **hello@itsthierry.com**.

**Your files stay on your phone,** so we cannot see them. If you want us to look at a problem, send the error log from the
app (Settings → Privacy), and look it over first: most dollar amounts are blanked out, but not every way of writing one.

**Moving to a new phone:** Settings → Backup makes a backup file you keep yourself. It holds your records, not the photos or
PDFs you attached.

**Pro:** manage or cancel from your store's subscriptions page. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android:
Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

Fairhold helps you spot problems; it does not guarantee that a document is genuine and is not legal or tax advice.
